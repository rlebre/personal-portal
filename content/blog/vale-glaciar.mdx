## The plan

The official Rota do Vale Glaciar is linear and climbs to the highest point of mainland Portugal. We wanted a loop instead, so we started in Manteigas at about 700 m and turned at Covão d'Ametade.

## The climb

Over 925 m of ascent spread over the day. The valley's U shape keeps the gradient honest, and the shade disappears after midday.

## Notes for next time

Start early, carry more water than you think, and check the weather in Manteigas, not in Aveiro.
