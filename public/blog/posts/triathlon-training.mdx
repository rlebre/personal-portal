# Scraping Together a Triathlon Training Plan

I signed up to compete in a half-Ironman™ triathlon on September 8, 2024. Completing a half-Ironman™ involves:

- 1.9 km swim
- 90 km bike
- 21.1 km run

In this post, I share how I used web scraping to gather training resources and build a custom plan.
